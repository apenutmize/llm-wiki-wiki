# Mark Molenaar

EdTech practitioner (ApeNutMize); this wiki's owner and maintainer, and co-author of
the Learning Impact Europe 2026 talk
[One Data, One Meaning](../sources/source-molenaar-one-data-one-meaning.md).

## Relevance to this wiki
- Owns the repository (`github.com/apenutmize/llm-wiki-wiki`, under the **ApeNutMize**
  org) and drives its ingestions.
- Authored, with [Lennart Nörenberg](lennart-norenberg.md), the talk applying the
  [LLM-wiki / OKF](../concepts/open-knowledge-format.md) pattern to learning
  analytics — proposing a [context dictionary](../concepts/context-dictionary.md)
  and an [OKF profile for EdTech](../concepts/okf-profile.md), with a
  [Caliper Analytics](caliper-analytics.md) proof of concept.
- The talk cites this repo as a source — a self-referential loop where the wiki is
  both the method and an artifact of the work.
