# Xavi Aracil

Technical Standards Architect at [1EdTech](1edtech.md) (xaracil@1edtech.org); author
of "The path to easier standards adoption — embracing new audiences."

## Relevance to this wiki
- Argues standards must be [AI-ready](../concepts/ai-ready-standards.md)
  (machine-readable, structured, traceable) so adopters learn them through AI with
  *trusted* answers — a standards-side statement of the LLM-wiki/OKF thesis.
- Described 1EdTech's [AI-enablers strategy](1edtech-ai-enablers.md) (microsites,
  MR-Specs, tooling) and the "[Working for Both Claudes](../concepts/provenance-and-trust.md)"
  human-plus-machine framing.
- Complements colleague [Tim Couper](tim-couper.md)'s [EduQuery](eduquery.md)
  proposal: Aracil covers *authoring/adoption*, Couper covers *runtime query*.
