# Vannevar Bush

American engineer and science administrator; in 1945 he described the **Memex**, a
personal knowledge machine built around associative trails between documents —
often cited as an intellectual ancestor of hypertext and the web.

## Relevance to this wiki
- His Memex vision is the historical lineage Joshi draws for the LLM-wiki pattern:
  the associative-trails idea whose only real obstacle was bookkeeping — the burden
  LLMs now absorb. See [Memex](../concepts/memex.md).
