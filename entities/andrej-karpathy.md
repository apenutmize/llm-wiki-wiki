# Andrej Karpathy

AI researcher and educator; the originator of the **LLM-wiki** framing this wiki
is built around. Known broadly for work in deep learning and for widely-followed
explainers on neural networks and language models.

## Relevance to this wiki
- Authored the seed document that defines the pattern:
  [LLM Wiki: Personal Knowledge Base Pattern](../sources/source-karpathy-llm-wiki-gist.md).
- His framing supplies the wiki's [three-layer architecture](../concepts/three-layer-architecture.md)
  and the [three operations](../concepts/three-operations.md).

## Origin (per Joshi's account)
- The pattern started as a tweet (Apr 2, 2026) on using LLMs to build personal
  knowledge bases, followed two days later by the `llm-wiki.md` gist — an "idea
  file" meant to be pasted into an agent to instantiate the pattern.
- Supplied the [compilation analogy](../concepts/knowledge-compilation-analogy.md)
  (raw sources = source code; wiki = binary) and, quoted by Joshi, the role split:
  "Obsidian is the IDE; the LLM is the programmer; the wiki is the codebase."
- His argument for why LLMs unlock this: they absorb the **bookkeeping** —
  updating cross-references, keeping summaries current — that makes humans abandon
  wikis. See [Memex](../concepts/memex.md).

## Downstream influence
- Google Cloud's [Open Knowledge Format](../concepts/open-knowledge-format.md)
  explicitly formalizes his LLM-wiki pattern into an open standard, and the
  announcement quotes his line that LLMs "don't get bored… and can touch 15 files in
  one pass" as the motivation for automated knowledge upkeep.
