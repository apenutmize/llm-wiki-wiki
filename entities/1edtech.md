# 1EdTech

Education-technology standards consortium (formerly IMS Global); steward of widely
used interoperability specs such as Caliper Analytics, OneRoster, LTI, QTI, and
CASE. Relevant here as the body whose specifications an
[OKF bundle of specs](../summaries/okf-for-1edtech-specs.md) would encode as a
shared semantic + conformance layer.

## Relevance to this wiki
- Author (via Chief Architect [Tim Couper](tim-couper.md)) of the
  [EduQuery proposal](../sources/source-1edtech-eduquery-ai-ready-query.md), which
  wants a "common abstract data model aligned with existing 1EdTech standards."
- Its multi-spec catalog is a textbook case of the
  [unified-semantics gap](../concepts/unified-semantics-gap.md): valuable standards
  that still diverge in implementation and meaning.
