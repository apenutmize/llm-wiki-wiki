# Cornelsen

One of the largest educational publishers in the German-speaking region (per the
talk, ~93% of students use Cornelsen media; 50 subjects, 300 syllabi, all 16 German
federal states). Founder Franz Cornelsen: "Education is the most important
investment in people."

## Relevance to this wiki
- Home of co-author [Lennart Nörenberg](lennart-norenberg.md); the publisher's stake
  in [learning analytics](../concepts/context-dictionary.md) motivates the talk's
  focus on shared *interpretation* of interoperable data.
