# Memex

The **Memex** is Vannevar Bush's 1945 vision of a personal, curated knowledge
store in which the **associative trails between documents** are as valuable as the
documents themselves. Joshi frames the LLM wiki as finally realizing it.

## Connection to the LLM wiki
- Bush's vision was private, actively curated, and link-centric — closer to a
  personal wiki than to what the public web became.
- It was never really built for a **non-technical** reason: nobody wants to do the
  bookkeeping — updating cross-references, keeping summaries current, noting
  contradictions.
- The LLM-wiki pattern removes exactly that barrier: the model absorbs the
  bookkeeping, so the associative-trails vision becomes practical. See
  [The three operations](three-operations.md) (the *lint* pass) and the
  [compilation analogy](knowledge-compilation-analogy.md).
